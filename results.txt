--- Phase 2: named biological checks (plan Sec 5.3) against `full`'s full-candidate predictions ---
hESC (00h_vs_96h, 00h_vs_12h, 36h_vs_72h): ALL FAIL TO RESOLVE. POU5F1/NANOG/SOX2 0% A-specific; SOX17/GATA4/GATA6 0-4.5% B-specific; EOMES 0% B-specific. Model predicts ~100% "neither" for these genes' out-edges in all 3 hESC pairs -- direct consequence of Phase 0/1's established hESC label-yield finding, not a new failure mode.
mESC naive-circuit collapse (0h_vs_72h, 0h_vs_12h): FAILS to cleanly resolve. All naive-circuit genes show <3% A-specific out-edges (mostly "neither"). Some genes (POU5F1, ESRRB, TBX3) show MORE B-specific than A-specific edges in 0h_vs_72h (opposite of predicted direction), though absolute percentages are small. OTX2 shows 0% B-specific (fails to confirm 72h-specific rise).
mHSC-E GATA2->GATA1 flagship edge: PARTIAL PASS. LT_HSC_vs_Prog: predicted "neither" (FAIL). HSPC_vs_Prog: predicted "B-specific" with 100% confidence (PASS) -- correctly flagged as strongly differential in exactly the pair the plan itself named as where the effect should be sharpest (erythroid-commitment step). Single most citable positive biological finding of Phase 2.
mHSC-E GATA2/SPI1/GFI1/GFI1B/EPOR broader enrichment checks: MOSTLY FAIL to confirm predicted direction, confounded by HSPC_vs_Prog's silver-label class skew (A-specific=0 training examples for that pair per Phase 0, so the model structurally cannot predict A-specific there at all -- limits how much can be concluded about gene-specific biology vs a general pair-level artifact). GFI1B in LT_HSC_vs_Prog is the one partial exception (99/257 out-edges "conserved", loosely consistent with predicted "conserved-active" pattern).
