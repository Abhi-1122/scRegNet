# W12 idea 4: Correct & Smooth on a graph of (TF, gene) pairs

hESC, 3 seeds (42-44), hero and hero + Geneformer. Reproduce: `python3.12 analysis/w12_cs.py` (post-hoc method, writes `analysis/out/w12_cs_{hero,herogf}.json`) then `python3.12 analysis/w12_4_report.py` (writes `analysis/out/w12_4_cs.txt`). Training runs: `analysis/jobs/w12_4_cs.txt` (`--save_sel_grid 1`).

## 1. Where the idea comes from

Correct & Smooth (Huang et al., ICLR 2021) improves a node classifier after training: the training residuals (label minus prediction) are propagated over the graph to correct errors that are spatially smooth ("correct"), then the corrected predictions are propagated with the known training labels held fixed ("smooth"). It needs no retraining and often lifts a simple model to the level of a GNN. Here the nodes are (TF, gene) pairs. The motivation was the repeated finding that late fusion and extra information about neighbouring pairs help (week 3), while retraining the GNN with new inputs did not.

## 2. Method in short

- Pair graph: (t,g) ~ (t,g') when g' is among the k nearest genes of g (k in {5, 10, 20}) in a gene graph that is either label-free co-expression correlation or cosine similarity of the model's own gene embeddings; and optionally (t,g) ~ (t',g) when t' is one of the 5 TFs with the largest Jaccard overlap of training targets with t. Edge types: gene edges, TF edges, or both (equal weight).
- Correct: E = (1-a1) R + a1 Op(E), R = y - sigmoid(score) on labelled training pairs and 0 elsewhere; p1 = p + s E. Smooth: Z = (1-a2) Z0 + a2 Op(Z), Z0 = p1 with training labels clamped. 30 iterations. Grid: graph (2) x k (3) x edge type (3) x a1 {0, 0.5, 0.8, 0.95} x s {0.5, 1, 2} x a2 {0, 0.5, 0.8, 0.95} (about 500 configurations).
- Leak control. `--save_sel_grid 1` saves (a) the full TF x gene score grid of the best-validation-epoch model (trained on the 90% training slice, graph = those edges), its gene embeddings, and (b) out-of-fold scores of every training row (20 folds; the fold's own edges are removed from the message-passing graph before scoring, so no training pair is scored through its own edge). The residuals use these out-of-fold scores. The same is saved for the refit model (all training rows).
- Selection on validation only. Per run, hyperparameters are chosen with the stage-1 model: training labels = the 90% slice, validation rows scored, criterion = validation AUROC (the metric that selects the GNN epoch). The same hyperparameters are applied to the refit model with all training labels, and the test set is scored once. A second criterion, mean(validation AUROC, validation within-TF), was also run and is reported.

Paired per-TF tests (33 TFs, per-TF AUROC averaged over seeds): hero + C&S minus hero within-TF -0.0144 (95% CI -0.044 to +0.009, 16/33 TFs better, p = 0.69); Geneformer hero + C&S minus Geneformer hero -0.0225 (CI -0.052 to 0.000, 9/33 better, p = 0.11). Hyperparameters picked on validation: coexpression graph, k = 10 (k = 5 for one seed), edge type "both" for two of three seeds, a1 in {0, 0.8, 0.95}, a2 in {0, 0.5}. Validation AUROC rose by only 0.0017 to 0.0031 (0.8887 -> 0.8904 for seed 42). With the mean criterion on the Geneformer hero, pooled test AUROC and AUPRC rise slightly (+0.0016, +0.0045) while within-TF falls by 0.0037.

Stage 2 and 3 were not run (regression in the primary metric, post-hoc method).

## 4. Why

Measured, hero, same hyperparameters with one part removed (test within-TF; raw 0.7488; n = number of seeds where the part exists):
- Correct only: 0.7487 (n = 2), 0.7499 for Geneformer (n = 2). The correct step does nothing.
- Smooth only: 0.7287 (n = 2), 0.7270 for Geneformer (n = 3). The smooth step carries the loss.
- Gene edges only: 0.7293; TF edges only: 0.7438. The loss comes from the gene-gene edges: label mass is spread to genes that merely look alike, which blurs the within-TF ranking.
- Score/popularity Spearman (over all genes, mean over TFs) falls from 0.732 to 0.689 (hero) and from 0.749 to 0.621 (Geneformer hero). A within-TF ranking by gene popularity alone reaches 0.707 within-TF AUROC (from `w12_1_query_tf.txt`), so popularity is a strong useful within-TF signal, and the smoothing removes part of it.
- Validation versus test disagree on within-TF. For seed 42 a configuration with TF edges only (a1 = 0.8) raised validation within-TF from 0.701 to 0.729 while its test within-TF is 0.729 against 0.750 raw. Validation holds about 9 positives per TF, so within-TF on that slice is noisy and the maximum over about 500 configurations is a winner's-curse pick. This is why validation AUROC is the primary criterion. It also picks configurations that lower the test within-TF (guess: pooled AUROC is dominated by the between-TF level, which the smoothing can nudge while hurting the ranking inside TFs; not tested).

Guess (not tested): the base model already encodes the neighbouring pairs' labels through the training graph (the TF embedding is a mean over known targets), so the residuals of neighbouring pairs carry little new information, and the clamped-label diffusion mainly adds noise from co-expression neighbours.

## 5. Verdict

Negative: C&S on the pair graph lowers within-TF by 0.014 (hero) and 0.022 (Geneformer hero); only pooled AUROC/AUPRC of the Geneformer hero moves up slightly under the mean criterion. Correct alone is neutral. If revisited: correct with TF-neighbour edges only, residuals from a held-out model, no smooth step.
