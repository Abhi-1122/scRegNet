xcell_gnn.no_labels(), which raises on any read of Train_set.csv, Test_set.csv, Label.csv, BL--network.csv; evaluate() then reads the target labels. Transfer runs assert target not in sources.

at most k neighbours; a perfect grid scores within-TF and pooled AUROC 1; TF-balanced weights sum to 1 per TF; a 2-epoch source+target consistency run completes under the guard.
